🗣️ YOUR SIX-STEP CONVERSATION GUIDE

1

Start the conversation

Pupil A: "I want to become a doctor in the future."

Pupil B: "That's interesting! Why do you want to become a doctor?"

2

Ask for advice

Pupil A: "What should I do to achieve my dream?"

3

Give advice

Pupil B: "I think you should study Science."

4

Explain the reason

Pupil B: "Because doctors need to understand the human body."

5

Continue the conversation

Pupil A: "Is there anything else I should do?"

Pupil B: "You should also learn how to communicate with people."

6

End politely

Pupil A: "Thank you for your advice!"

Pupil B: "You're welcome! I hope you achieve your dream!"

💡 CAREER-SPECIFIC ADVICE

You should study Science because doctors need to understand the human body.

🌱 ANOTHER HELPFUL SUGGESTION

LEVEL 5 • SPEAK WITH CONFIDENCE

💬 COMMUNICATION TOOLKIT

Open each card to find useful expressions for your conversation.

🙋 A. Asking for Advice
  • What should I do?
  • Can you give me some advice?
  • How can I achieve my dream?
💡 B. Giving Advice
  • You should…
  • You should not…
  • I think you should…
  • Why don't you…?
🔍 C. Giving Reasons
  • Because…
  • This is important because…
  • It can help you…
  • This is a good idea because…
❓ D. Follow-up Questions
  • Is there anything else I should do?
  • Can you give me another suggestion?
  • Why do you think so?
😊 E. Responding Politely
  • That's a good idea!
  • I agree with you.
  • Thank you for your advice!
  • You're welcome!
👂 F. Taking Turns
  • What do you think?
  • What is your advice?
  • Can I share another idea?
